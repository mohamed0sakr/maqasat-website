Engineering engine

Engineering truth that travels with the job.

MAQASAT treats a window, door or fabrication model as a parametric engineering structure — not a static drawing or a hard-coded price line.

Family→Sector→Shared Rules→Model→W × H→Profiles→Glass→Accessories→BOM→Cutting

Sector & Model Engine

Aluminum/UPVC families, hinged and sliding structures, dimensions, component quantities, glass, accessories and topology relationships.

Engineering Snapshots

Documents can retain the engineering truth/version used when created, avoiding silent historical changes.

Owner Engineering Studio

Owner-only governance for models, rules, inheritance/overrides, validation, preview, lifecycle and publishing.

Smart Kitchen

One kitchen model. Every downstream output.

The Smart Kitchen architecture is deterministic and parametric at its core. AI may assist, but it does not replace engineering truth.

Manual, Assisted and Auto Design; room geometry, walls, openings, utilities and appliances; parametric base, upper, tall, corner, appliance, island and decorative units; full editing; constraint logic for clearances, safety, symmetry and conflicts; and one canonical model shared by 2D, 3D, BOM, cutting, quotation and PDFs.

Materials intelligence

A closed material loop — from requirement to reusable remnant.

01 Requirement

02 Stock + Remnants
Check stock and usable remnants first.
03 Net Purchase
Requirement minus available, remnants and reserved.
04 1D / 2D Cutting
Optimize rods and sheets for lower waste.
05 Return & Trace
Reusable remnants return to inventory with job attribution.

1D Rod Optimization

Rod lengths, required cuts, kerf, existing remnants, optimization and reusable resulting lengths.

2D Panel Optimization

Sheets, panels and fiber with dimensions, constraints, nesting and reusable offcuts.
